📖 How to Play

Welcome to Golf on Stream! It's like marbles on stream but mini golf!

Engage your Twitch viewers in an interactive mini-golf adventure driven entirely by chat commands. Your viewers control the balls directly from your twitch chat!

Commands

  • !play : Join the course
  • shoot <dir> <pow> : Take a shot
    e.g. shoot n 10 or shoot 45 5
  • !repeat : Re-take your previous shot
